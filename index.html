About Me

I’m a web developer and graphic designer with a strong interest in computers, technology, and digital creativity. I enjoy working at the intersection of design and code, where I can take ideas from concept to execution and build experiences that are both visually engaging and technically sound.

My interest in technology has been shaped by a lifelong appreciation for science fiction, especially Star Wars, which inspires my love for immersive worlds, storytelling, and thoughtful design. Alongside that, working with Lego has influenced how I approach problem-solving and structure: breaking complex ideas into smaller parts and assembling them into something cohesive, functional, and creative. A little fun fact, I personally own the 6th biggest Lego set every made, the UCS Millennium Falcon.

As an aspiring designer and developer, I’m eager to gain as much hands-on experience as possible and continue learning in a professional environment. I’m motivated, detail-oriented, and excited to contribute creatively while developing my skills in both front-end development and design. I’m looking for opportunities where I can grow, collaborate, and help bring compelling digital ideas to life.
